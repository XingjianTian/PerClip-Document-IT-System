Read this page first, then open the shared Google Docs script and find your named section. You do not need a development environment or a running system to review documents and prepare the presentation. GitHub supports reading and version storage; Google Docs supports collaborative editing.

## 1. What is our project?

**PerClip RAG Document Management System** falls under **Document Management System** in Appendix A. The proposed English-language Android interface will connect document capture, metadata organisation, archiving and retrieval. RAG questions and source references are planned capabilities; basic saving, title search and detail access should remain usable when RAG services are unavailable.

These materials are project planning drafts. Goals, features, effort, budgets and acceptance criteria are plans, not evidence of completed development, passed tests or measured benefits. Everyone should review, contribute to and explain their assigned content.

## 4. What each member should do now

1. Read your named chapter in the shared script, then all three formal documents to understand the overall project logic.
2. Check your name. Matriculation numbers, contacts, actual skills and meeting availability are blank for each member to provide. Meeting venue, platform and team commitments also need actual confirmation.
3. Check your assigned content for clarity, consistent numbers and feasible scope. Suggest specific comments in the formal Google Docs, explaining reasons and impacts.
4. After changes, check your script section too. Notify other relevant owners when budget, MOV, scope or dates change. Google Docs and GitHub do not sync automatically; the leader updates repository versions after integrating and confirming revisions.
5. Explain your section in your own words, then rehearse the English script. Prepare to explain why decisions were made, rather than only reading aloud. Prepare at least two likely questions about your section and read the [Q&A draft](presentation/04_Typical_QA_EN.md).
6. Rehearse the full presentation together in order. Check handovers, names, numbers and repetition. Confirm the final version after actual contributions and reviews are complete.

The requirements specify one submission per group and list documents and a presentation. Every member must participate; rubrics assess answers to questions. The listed deliverables do not explicitly require source code or an APK. This does not mean merely reading a script is sufficient.

**The original text neither explicitly requires a live presentation nor permits a recorded video to replace it.** Separate screen-and-voice recordings combined into one video are a candidate approach, to be produced only after the lecturer confirms acceptance and rules. Prepare everyone to explain and answer questions now. A selected full-group script should be rehearsed for a suggested 8–10 minutes, excluding Q&A; this is not a lecturer requirement.

Google Docs supports collaboration; bilingual Markdown supports reference and version storage. This is the group’s arrangement, not a lecturer-specified submission format. Check current course requirements for final format, naming, submission entry and acceptance of video. Completing Google Docs does not constitute a course submission.

## 6. Shared figures and wording

- Planned effort: **90 person-hours, 18 hours per member**, for course planning, review and presentation preparation. This does not estimate production system development.
- Estimated budget: shadow labour **MYR 2,250** (90 × MYR 25/h) + optional allowance **MYR 100** + contingency **MYR 235** = **MYR 2,585**. These are not actual expenditure or confirmed funding.
- Draft MOV: a future five-person test with a fixed fictional document collection, aiming for at least four people to find a specified document within **60 seconds**. No test results exist; performance comparisons require a recorded baseline.
- Describe features and acceptance using proposed / planned / will. Do not present targets as measured results, sign for others, or invent meetings or personal skills.
