## 4. Using the single complete script

Use only the [Final Presentation Script](01_Final_Presentation_Script_EN.md). All five collaborate in one shared Google Docs document, with named chapters. Each member’s content separates A (Proposal/Team Charter) and B (Project Charter). Use B as the main content and select background/team arrangements from A as needed. These are not two speeches and do not require two separately recorded sets.

Do not read all of A and B consecutively. After selecting content, remove repeated introductions, numbers and conclusions. Retain one handover per member, at the end of their section. The leader opens once and returns once after ZHANG to conclude. Do not read headings or preparation notes aloud.

Rehearse the selected full-group version for a suggested 8–10 minutes, excluding Q&A. Adjust individual sections using measured speaking speed. This is not a lecturer-prescribed duration; follow any lecturer instructions instead. Retain participation by all five and key rubric coverage. Task 1 and Task 2 remain separate written submissions despite the combined script.

## 5. Recording workflow after lecturer acceptance

1. Everyone understands their own content and uses consistent project/team names and proposed / will wording.
2. Revise the script against the actual final documents. Keep one consistent budget and MOV version; leave meeting-time fields blank.
3. Prepare a clear prompting script, breaking lines at complete sentences. The original does not explicitly prohibit reading a script, but everyone should be able to explain concepts without it.
4. Make a 15-second test recording in a quiet setting. Check voice, lighting, picture and screen text; use landscape orientation and similar volume. Follow course requirements for the actual format.
5. Prefer a continuous five-person recording. If recording separately, retain natural transition sentences. Each person states their name; the image or captions identify their actual name and section.
6. Combine in this order: XINGJIAN opening → CHUN → LIN → TAN → ZHANG → XINGJIAN conclusion, with short pauses between sentences. Do not edit together fabricated meetings, discussions or live Q&A.
7. Watch the full video on a phone and computer. Check all five voices, captions, order, duration, frame edges and the complete ending.
8. Obtain content confirmation from all five; save the final video and editable script. An authorised member submits according to course requirements. Materials in this directory do not constitute submission.

## 7. Five-person Q&A rehearsal

Everyone prepares role-specific and cross-role questions. Answer directly in two or three English sentences, then explain the basis or outstanding issue. State when you do not know; never invent numbers on the spot. The main owner may add detail, but do not send every question to the leader.

- XINGJIAN: Why is this a Document Management System? How are stakeholders and the team coordinated? How are changes recorded and approved? Cross-role practice: how is MOV verified?
- CHUN: What are the scope boundaries and AI trade-offs? How do MOV targets differ from results? Cross-role practice: how is the budget estimated?
- LIN: How is the WBS broken down? Which tasks change when a deadline moves? Cross-role practice: how is acceptance assessed?
- TAN: How is MYR 2,585 calculated, and why is it not expenditure? How will the highest-priority risk be addressed? Cross-role practice: how do risks affect milestones?
- ZHANG: How do quality criteria map to test evidence? Who has acceptance authority? Cross-role practice: how is MOV defined?

Use the companion [Q&A draft](04_Typical_QA_EN.md) for practice answers. Do not describe preparation as meetings already held or tests already completed. Take turns drawing questions; everyone practises at least two. This is suggested practice, not a record of an event.
