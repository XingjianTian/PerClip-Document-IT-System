### B — Project Charter content

Thank you, Xingjian. I am Chun Ze Li. My proposed role covers requirements, scope, and measurable organisational value.

Our proposed MOV is that, in a future five-person test, at least four participants will find a specified document within sixty seconds using a fixed fictional collection. We will record each participant’s completion and retrieval time. The collection and task will stay consistent across participants. Any requested addition should be assessed for value, time, cost, and risk before changing that baseline. Lin will now describe the proposed schedule and its dependencies.
