## (How to communicate to share information)

Proposed communication: a private group chat for coordination, GitHub issues or a shared action list for assigned work, and pull requests for document review. The team must choose the chat platform at its first meeting. A chat decision affecting scope, budget or deadlines is copied into the decision log.

Members should acknowledge a work request within 24 hours where possible. A blocker threatening a deadline should be raised the same day. The leader coordinates responses and escalates unresolved course-requirement questions to the lecturer only after the group agrees on the wording. One nominated representative submits the academic deliverables once for the group.

## Team Goals

Produce a coherent Proposal and Team Charter for Task 1, a complete Project Charter for Task 2, and a clear presentation involving all five members. Keep PerClip RAG Document Management System within a practical document-management scope and support later prototype claims with actual evidence.

## Set Team Values

Work transparently, respect availability and credit actual contributions. Use clear English terminology, review work constructively and keep feature plans, targets and measured results distinct.

## Set Team Code Of Ethics

Do not fabricate interviews, user tests, expenditure, development results, attendance, agreements or signatures. Cite course and external sources where used. Review AI-assisted writing for accuracy, follow applicable university rules and disclose assistance if required. Keep private documents and credentials out of the repository and recordings.

## Set Rules And Expectations

Owners draft before internal review dates; reviewers return actionable feedback within 24 hours where possible. The leader checks integration, but each owner remains responsible for accuracy. Accept a change only after documenting its effect on scope, time, cost and quality. Seek consensus; unresolved differences are recorded with options and referred to the lecturer when needed.

For missed tasks, contact the owner, agree a recovery date and reassign only through an explicit group decision. Record actual work rather than assuming equal contribution. Before recording, everyone rehearses their own section, verifies terminology and prepares answers. A composite video remains conditional on lecturer acceptance; all members remain available for live questions.
